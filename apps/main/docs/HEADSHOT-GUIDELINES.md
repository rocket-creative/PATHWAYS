# Headshot Photography Guidelines - Pathways Within

## Background Style Requirements

All staff headshots should feature a **soft, blurred botanical/greenery background** for visual consistency across the team.

### Reference Examples
- ✅ **Alexa Cordeiro headshot 2** - Perfect example (soft green plants, professional blur)
- ✅ **Briana Nelson headshot 1** - Perfect example (botanical background, warm lighting)

### Required Background Elements
1. **Greenery/Plants** - Soft-focus plants, foliage, or office greenery
2. **Depth of Field** - Shallow depth (f/2.8 or wider) to blur background
3. **Natural/Warm Tones** - Soft lighting, warm color temperature
4. **Soft Bokeh** - Beautiful out-of-focus areas
5. **Consistent Placement** - Subject centered, showing from shoulders up

### Photo Specifications
- **Aspect Ratio**: Square (1:1) or portrait (3:4)
- **Resolution**: Minimum 1200x1200px
- **Format**: JPEG or PNG
- **File Size**: Under 500KB (optimized for web)
- **Framing**: Head and shoulders, eyes visible, warm smile
- **Background**: Blurred botanical elements (plants, greenery)

### Photography Setup
**Camera Settings:**
- Aperture: f/1.8 - f/2.8 (for background blur)
- Focal Length: 50mm - 85mm (portrait lens)
- ISO: 100-400 (natural light)
- Position plants 4-6 feet behind subject

**Location Ideas:**
- Near office plants/greenery
- Indoor atrium with plants
- Professional setup with potted plants
- Outdoor botanical setting (overcast day for soft light)
