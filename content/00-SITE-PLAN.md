## Content rules reminder

### Voice
- Warm, professional, authoritative, approachable, compassionate
- Never clinical, cold, preachy, urgent, or salesy

### Copy standards
- NO HYPHENS (rewrite to avoid)
- Active voice preferred
- Sentence case for headings
- Oxford comma always
- 8th grade reading level target

### Forbidden language
- "Spa," "medspa," "med spa," "medical spa" — use "wellness services"
- "Sister site" or "two paths" — it's ONE collaborative
- "Choose your path" — use "begin your journey"
- Guarantees of outcomes
- Ableist language
- Urgency or pressure tactics

### Required elements on every page
- 988 Crisis Lifeline (visible)
- Footer with privacy/terms links
- Cookie consent
