## Our philosophy section

**Body:**

## The 360 degree approach section

**Headline:** How we care for you

**Body:**
We take a 360 degree approach to healing, looking at both your mental and physical well being. During your intake, we explore what brings you in, how long it has been going on, and how it affects areas like sleep, stress, digestion, energy, pain, and relationships. We also discuss what you have already tried.

Based on this conversation, we may suggest somatic or supportive services that could enhance your wellness journey. These recommendations are always optional and offered only when we feel they may truly support you.

From there, we create a personalized treatment plan, help you schedule next steps or a follow up, and coordinate with your care team to ensure thoughtful, ongoing support.

## What makes us different section

**Headline:** Why we are different

### Integrated care
Unlike practices that offer only therapy or only body care, we bring both together. Your therapist and wellness providers can collaborate to support your complete well being.

### Personalized approach
The journey within should be as personal and individual as you are. We look to create an experience for each person that is uniquely theirs.

### Expert team
Our therapists are trained, licensed, and experienced in different areas of counseling. Each clinician works with special populations based on their expertise and passions. Our wellness providers bring years of experience in their specialties.

### Accessible care
We accept most major insurance for both therapy and applicable wellness services. Sliding scale options are available based on financial need. We also offer telehealth across multiple states.

## Our story section

**Headline:** Our story

**Body:**
Pathways Within was founded with a vision of bringing together mental health care and holistic wellness under one roof. We saw how often people needed support for both their minds and their bodies, yet had to seek that care in separate places with providers who never communicated.

Our founder, Rachel Lessard, LCSW R, built Pathways Within to change that. With certifications spanning clinical social work, energy work, and integrative wellness practices, Rachel created a sanctuary where clients can heal, grow, and embrace their most authentic self.

Today, our team has grown to include licensed therapists, certified wellness providers, and compassionate support staff across five Long Island locations. We continue to honor that founding vision: one collaborative environment where your whole self is welcome.
