## Virtual services section

**Headline:** Can not make it in person?

**Body:** We offer virtual therapy appointments through SimplePractice, our HIPAA compliant telehealth platform. Virtual sessions are available for clients in New York, New Jersey, North Carolina, and Florida.

Before your first virtual visit, we will send you a link to get set up. During this onboarding process, you will have the chance to get familiar with our platform.

**Is virtual therapy private?** Absolutely. SimplePractice is both HIPAA compliant and VeriSign security sealed. Your privacy is crucial to us, and we take every step to ensure confidentiality in every format.
