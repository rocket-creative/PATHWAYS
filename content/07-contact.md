## What to expect section

**Headline:** What happens next?

**Body:**
After you reach out, a member of our team will contact you within one business day to discuss your needs and answer any questions. We will help you:

1. Understand your options for therapy and wellness services
2. Verify your insurance benefits (if applicable)
3. Find the right provider for your needs
4. Schedule your first appointment

There is no pressure and no obligation. This is simply a conversation to help you decide if Pathways Within is right for you.

## Crisis resources section

**Headline:** Need immediate help?

If you are experiencing suicidal thoughts or feel at risk of harm, please seek help immediately. Do not wait for an appointment.

**988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline**
Call, text, or chat
Veterans: Press 1
Spanish: Presione 2

**Crisis Text Line:** Text HOME to 741741

**NYC Well:** 888 NYC WELL (888 692 9355)

**Emergency:** Call 911
