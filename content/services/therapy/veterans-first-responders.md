## Understanding section

**Headline:** Speaking up is not weak

**Body:**
The very experiences that set you apart as an honorable part of our society can make it feel difficult to participate in society as a whole person when you feel like you have been taken apart.

We want to support you to find your pathways within, back to your true self and all the parts that make up the hero you are perceived as. With experts trained in the tools and therapies designed to support veterans and first responders, we are here and ready to help.

Asking for help is a show of the very strength and bravery you are known for.

### Therapy can help with:

- Post Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)
- Anxiety or panic attacks
- Depression
- Isolation or feelings of detachment
- Difficulty regulating your mood
- Substance abuse

You do not need to be able to identify the support you need to be deserving of receiving it. We are here to help you heal, and part of that journey is navigating what you have experienced and where you are still stuck.

Trauma is not linear, and neither is healing. No matter where you are on your journey, we can find our way forward together.

## For veterans section

**Headline:** For veterans

**Body:**
The life of a service member is unique. While you are serving, you remain busy with frequent relocations and unique obligations that make it hard to connect with a sense of community outside the military. Once you leave, the loss of that community offers you roots but may have you feeling ungrounded.

When coupled with the high instances of stress and crisis exposure, veterans are at a higher risk for depression, loneliness, and isolation even after their time is served.

We offer tools to support you in overcoming what you have been through and preparing for what you may experience in the future. Through therapeutic experiences tailored to your lifestyle, needs, and past, we will cultivate a unique plan consisting of therapies like talk therapy, EMDR, or IET to ensure you feel you have got a plan in place to heal and manage whatever may come your way.

## For first responders section

**Headline:** For first responders

**Body:**
Crisis response is a part of your job. Every day, you are facing things that reshape the lives of those you encounter. Your work, your presence, and your impact are felt in the "once in a lifetime" moments that you face time and again, yet each one is as unique as the mark it will leave on you.

You are not bad at your job if you are struggling to reconcile the things you have seen or the way you feel. Leaving work at work is not an option for first responders, and we want to ensure you have got the tools you need to draw boundaries that allow you to find peace in your life and healing in the trauma often left behind by such formative work.

Whether you are working in everyday front line service or are trained to respond to disasters and unprecedented events, you are more than your job. Your mental health matters and we want to support you.

## For both section

**Headline:** When you are both

**Body:**
When you have had multiple high stress, crisis facing roles in your life, you may feel both defined by them and divided by them.

The American Psychological Association has indicated a high crossover in modalities and therapeutic experience style for veterans and first responders. We combine a variety of therapeutic styles to create a custom plan just for you, designed with you in mind and tailored to your needs.

Your healing journey may include puzzling together the shape of your peace, but there is one thing we can always guarantee: you will have a voice in not only your healing but how we work toward it.
