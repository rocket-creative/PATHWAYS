## History and science section

**Headline:** The history and science of acupuncture

**Body:**
Acupuncture is a traditional Chinese medicine practice that dates back over 2,500 years. It is based on the concept that health and wellness come from the balanced flow of energy, known as Qi (pronounced "chee"), throughout the body. When this energy is blocked or out of balance, illness and discomfort can result.

Modern research has shown that acupuncture stimulates the nervous system and releases endorphins, which are natural pain relieving chemicals produced by the body. This ancient practice has been shown to be effective in treating a wide range of health conditions.

## FAQ section

**Headline:** Frequently asked questions

### Is acupuncture safe?
Yes, acupuncture is a safe practice when performed by a qualified and licensed acupuncturist. We use sterile, single use needles to ensure your safety.

### Does acupuncture hurt?
Most clients report that acupuncture is not painful. The needles used are very thin and are inserted gently. You may feel a slight sensation when the needle is inserted, but this quickly fades.

### How many sessions will I need?
The number of sessions needed depends on your individual condition and health goals. Some clients experience relief after just one session, while others may need multiple sessions. Our acupuncturist will work with you to develop a treatment plan.

### How long does each session last?
A typical acupuncture session lasts about 30 to 45 minutes, including consultation and treatment.

### What should I expect during my first session?
During your first session, we will discuss your health history and goals. The acupuncturist will then perform an assessment and create a customized treatment plan for you.

### Can acupuncture be combined with other treatments?
Yes, acupuncture can be combined with other wellness treatments to enhance results. Many clients benefit from combining acupuncture with massage, energy work, or other therapies.
