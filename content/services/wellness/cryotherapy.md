## What is cryotherapy section

**Headline:** What is cryotherapy?

**Body:**
Cryotherapy is a wellness treatment that exposes the body to extremely cold temperatures (around negative 200 degrees Fahrenheit) for a short period of time (two to three minutes). This cold exposure stimulates the body's natural healing response, reducing inflammation and promoting overall wellness.

---

## How it works section

**Headline:** How does cryotherapy work?

**Body:**
When exposed to extreme cold, the body's natural response is to redirect blood flow from the extremities to the core to protect vital organs.

Once the exposure ends, blood rapidly returns to the extremities, bringing nutrient rich, oxygenated blood that promotes healing and reduces inflammation.

## The process section

**Headline:** The cryotherapy process

1. **Preparation:** You will change into minimal clothing and remove any metal jewelry.
2. **Chamber entry:** You will enter the cryotherapy chamber, which will gradually cool down.
3. **Exposure:** The chamber reaches extreme cold temperatures for two to three minutes.
4. **Exit:** The chamber warms up and you exit feeling energized.
5. **Recovery:** Most people report feeling the benefits within 15 to 30 minutes.

## FAQ section

**Headline:** Frequently asked questions

### Is cryotherapy safe?
Yes, whole body cryotherapy is safe when performed by trained professionals. The exposure time is brief, and the chamber is carefully monitored.

### How cold does the chamber get?
The chamber reaches temperatures around negative 200 degrees Fahrenheit (negative 130 degrees Celsius), but you are protected from frostbite by the brief exposure time.

### How often should I do cryotherapy?
For athletic recovery, three to five times per week is recommended. For general wellness, one to two times per week is beneficial.

### What should I expect to feel?
Most people report feeling invigorated and energized immediately after treatment. Over time, clients notice reduced inflammation and improved recovery.

### Can everyone use cryotherapy?
Cryotherapy is safe for most people, but certain conditions may make it inadvisable. We recommend consulting with our specialists if you have any health concerns.

### How long do the benefits last?
Acute benefits like improved mood and energy can last several hours. Anti inflammatory effects build with regular treatments.
