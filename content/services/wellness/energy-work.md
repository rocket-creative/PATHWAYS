## FAQ section

**Headline:** Frequently asked questions

### What is energy work?
Energy work is a holistic practice that works with the body's subtle energy system to promote healing and balance. It is based on the belief that all living things have life force energy that can be channeled and directed for healing purposes.

### Is energy work safe?
Yes, energy work is a safe and noninvasive practice. It does not involve any physical manipulation or medication.

### What should I expect during a session?
During an energy work session, you will relax in a comfortable environment while the practitioner channels healing energy. Most sessions last 30 to 60 minutes.

### Can energy work be combined with other treatments?
Yes, energy work complements many other wellness treatments and can be used alongside conventional medical treatments.

### Will I feel anything during the session?
Some clients report feeling warmth, tingling, or other sensations during energy work, while others may not feel anything physical but report feeling emotionally or spiritually shifted afterward.
