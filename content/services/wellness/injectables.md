## Botox section

**Headline:** Botox

**Body:**
Botox is a popular injectable treatment that uses botulinum toxin to relax facial muscles and smooth wrinkles caused by repetitive facial expressions. Results are natural looking and typically appear within three to seven days, with full results visible at two weeks.

### Benefits of Botox

- Smooths forehead lines
- Reduces crow's feet
- Minimizes frown lines
- Prevents new wrinkles from forming
- Results appear gradually for a natural look

## Dermal fillers section

**Headline:** Dermal fillers

**Body:**
Dermal fillers use hyaluronic acid or other materials to restore lost volume, smooth lines, and enhance facial features. They are ideal for adding fullness to lips, cheeks, and under eye areas.

### Types of fillers

- Hyaluronic acid fillers (Juvederm, Restylane)
- Calcium hydroxylapatite fillers
- Poly L lactic acid fillers
- Autologous fat transfer

### Benefits of fillers

- Restores lost volume
- Smooths smile lines and nasolabial folds
- Enhances lips
- Plumps cheeks
- Nonsurgical alternative to facelifts

## Kybella section

**Headline:** Kybella

**Body:**
Kybella is an injectable treatment designed to reduce the appearance of submental fullness (double chin). It contains deoxycholic acid, which breaks down fat cells under the chin.

### Benefits of Kybella

- Reduces double chin appearance
- Nonsurgical alternative to liposuction
- Natural looking results
- Long lasting results

## FAQ section

**Headline:** Frequently asked questions

### Are injectables safe?
Yes, when administered by trained medical professionals using FDA approved products, injectables are a safe and effective treatment option.

### How long do results last?
Botox typically lasts three to four months, while fillers can last six to eighteen months depending on the type used.

### Is there downtime?
Minimal downtime with injectables. You may experience slight bruising or swelling, but can return to normal activities immediately.

### Can I combine different injectables?
Yes, combining Botox with fillers can create a more comprehensive rejuvenation effect.

### What if I do not like the results?
Results appear gradually, allowing you to see how you like them. If you are not satisfied, most effects are reversible or can be adjusted at a follow up appointment.

### How much do injectables cost?
Cost varies depending on the type and amount of product used. We recommend scheduling a consultation for personalized pricing.
