## FAQ section

**Headline:** Frequently asked questions

### Is KeraLase safe?
Yes, KeraLase is a safe and noninvasive treatment. It uses low level laser energy that has been clinically proven to be safe for hair restoration.

### How long before I see results?
Some clients notice improvement in hair thickness and growth within two to three months, though full results typically take six months or longer.

### Can KeraLase treat severe hair loss?
KeraLase is most effective for those experiencing early to moderate hair loss. For severe hair loss, we recommend consulting with our specialists about additional treatment options.

### Is there downtime?
No, KeraLase has no downtime. You can return to your regular activities immediately after treatment.

### Can men and women both use KeraLase?
Yes, KeraLase is safe and effective for both men and women experiencing hair loss or thinning.

### What results can I expect?
Results vary depending on individual factors, but many clients report improved hair thickness, reduced hair loss, and new hair growth.
