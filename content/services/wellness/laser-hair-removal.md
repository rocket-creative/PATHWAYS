## What is laser hair removal section

**Headline:** What is laser hair removal?

**Body:**
Laser hair removal is a noninvasive cosmetic procedure that uses concentrated beams of light to target and destroy hair follicles, preventing future hair growth. It is an effective, safe, and long lasting solution for unwanted hair.

---

## How it works section

**Headline:** How does laser hair removal work?

**Body:**
During a laser hair removal session, a laser device is applied to the skin. The laser emits concentrated light energy that is absorbed by the pigment in the hair follicles. This energy destroys the follicle, preventing future hair growth from that follicle.

## Treatment process section

**Headline:** Treatment process

A typical laser hair removal treatment requires six to eight sessions spaced four to six weeks apart, depending on hair type, skin tone, and the area being treated. Each session typically lasts 15 to 60 minutes depending on the size of the area.

## FAQ section

**Headline:** Frequently asked questions

### Is laser hair removal safe?
Yes, laser hair removal is a safe, FDA approved procedure when performed by trained professionals using appropriate equipment and protocols.

### Does laser hair removal hurt?
Most clients report minimal discomfort, often describing the sensation as a slight snapping feeling. We can apply numbing cream before treatment if desired.

### How many sessions will I need?
Most clients need six to eight sessions, but this varies depending on factors like hair type, skin tone, and the area being treated.

### How long do results last?
Results are long lasting, with most clients enjoying permanently reduced hair growth. Some may need occasional maintenance treatments.

### Can laser hair removal be used on all skin types?
Modern laser technology can be used on most skin types. We recommend scheduling a consultation to determine if you are a good candidate.

### What should I do to prepare?
Avoid sun exposure and tanning for two weeks before treatment. Shave the area beforehand but avoid waxing or plucking.
