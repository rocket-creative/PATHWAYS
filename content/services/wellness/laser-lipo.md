## How it works section

**Headline:** How laser lipo works

**Body:**
The chain of events that occur beneath the surface of your skin while you relax and let laser lipo sculpt your confidence is fascinating. So many things take place that you never see.

When you arrive, you will be taken into one of our treatment rooms and prepared for your service. Your experience begins when the laser lipo paddles are applied to the targeted area.

Penetrating deeply and harmlessly through the upper layers of your skin, the specially designed lasers exclusively target fat cells called adipocyte cells that rapidly respond to the new intrusion.

The lasers create pores in the cells that allow them to sneak through barrier layers and damage the cell's stability. As the laser sneaks in, the water, fat, and glycogen that keep fat cells healthy will spill out into the surrounding tissue. The body will reabsorb them, shrinking the cells as they lose what shapes them.

With none of these elements to refuel them, the fat cells lose their power and are banished.

## FAQ section

**Headline:** Frequently asked questions

### How long does it take?
Each area treated takes 30 minutes and has the potential to remove one to four inches from the treated area. You will see results immediately, but they will also continue to develop over time. Your treatment will be the gift that keeps on giving for up to six weeks of transformation.

### How many sessions will I need?
You will see results from just your first session, but if there are other areas you would like to treat or more contouring you want to see, we can curate custom packages to meet your needs. From a single session to an ongoing plan, we will meet you where you would like!

### Who benefits most from laser lipo?
Those looking to trim and change a body that has already been changing are ideal candidates for laser lipo. Your results will be best if you have experienced some weight loss or are within 25 pounds of your desired body shape. Laser lipo can reinforce the confidence you are working toward already.

### What are the risks?
There are minimal risks with laser lipo. It is noninvasive, so no cutting or downtime to interfere with a busy schedule. You may experience some tenderness at the site of the treatment or feel a bit sore for the days after. Some people feel dehydrated or experience minor swelling, so staying on top of your water intake and treating yourself to some relaxation may help you see the best results after treatment.
