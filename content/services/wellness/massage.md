## Types of massage section

**Headline:** Types of massage

### Swedish massage
A classic massage technique that uses long, flowing strokes to promote relaxation and improve circulation. Swedish massage is perfect for those new to massage therapy or anyone looking for a relaxing experience.

### Deep tissue massage
A more intense massage that targets deeper layers of muscle tissue. Deep tissue massage is ideal for those with chronic pain or muscle tension.

### Sports massage
Designed for athletes and active individuals, sports massage helps to prevent injuries, improve performance, and speed up recovery.

### Hot stone massage
This luxurious massage uses heated stones to warm muscles and promote deep relaxation. Hot stone massage is perfect for those looking to melt away stress and tension.

### Pregnancy massage
Specially designed for expectant mothers, pregnancy massage helps to reduce pregnancy related discomfort and promote relaxation during this special time.

### Relaxation massage
A gentle, soothing massage designed to promote relaxation and reduce stress. Perfect for anyone looking to unwind and recharge.

## FAQ section

**Headline:** Frequently asked questions

### How long should my massage be?
A typical massage session is 60 minutes, but we also offer 30 minute express sessions and 90 minute deep dive sessions.

### How often should I get a massage?
This depends on your individual needs and goals. Some clients benefit from weekly sessions, while others prefer monthly or every other week treatments. Our therapists can recommend a schedule that works best for you.

### Is massage safe?
Yes, massage is a safe and effective therapy for most people. However, if you have any health concerns, we recommend consulting with your healthcare provider before scheduling a massage.

### What should I expect during my first massage?
During your first massage, we will take time to understand your needs and goals. We will discuss any areas of tension or pain and customize the massage to address your specific concerns.

### What should I wear?
You will be draped with sheets and blankets during your massage to ensure your comfort and privacy. Most clients prefer to remove their clothing, but you can wear as much as you are comfortable with.

### Do I need to do anything to prepare?
Arrive a few minutes early to fill out a health form. Avoid eating a large meal right before your massage, and try to arrive in a relaxed state of mind.

### Is there anything I should avoid after my massage?
After your massage, we recommend avoiding strenuous activity for at least 24 hours. Drink plenty of water to help flush out toxins, and try to rest and relax as much as possible.
