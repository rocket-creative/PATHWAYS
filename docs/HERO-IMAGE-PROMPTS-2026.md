# Freepik AI Image Prompts - Hero Background Images

## Quick Reference

| Spec | Value |
|------|-------|
| **Aspect Ratio** | **16:9 Landscape** (horizontal) |
| **Dimensions** | 1920 × 1080 px (or 2560 × 1440 px for hi-res) |
| **Composition** | **People RIGHT-JUSTIFIED** (subjects on right side of frame) |
| **Left Side** | **Clean/empty space** for logo and text overlay |

| **Text** | **NO TEXT IN IMAGES** |

## Key Composition Rules

### CRITICAL: Subject Positioning
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                     │
│   LOGO & TEXT        │    SUBJECTS/PEOPLE          │
│   OVERLAY AREA       │    POSITIONED HERE          │
│   (Clean white       │    (Right 2/3 of frame)     │
│    space)            │                             │
│                      │                             │
│   Left 1/3           │    Right 2/3                │
│                                                     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Subjects:** Always on RIGHT side of frame
- **Left third:** Clean white space (NO subjects here)
- **Background:** Pure white studio, no gradients
- **Aspect:** 16:9 landscape (1920×1080 or 2560×1440)

## Tips for Best Results

### DO:
✅ Generate multiple variations of each prompt  
✅ Pick images where people look natural, not stiff  
✅ Ensure good diversity (age, ethnicity, body type)  
✅ Check that clothing colors feel harmonious but not identical  
✅ Look for genuine expressions, not forced smiles  
✅ **Verify subjects are on the RIGHT side of frame**
✅ **Confirm left third is clean white for text overlay**
✅ **Verify pure white background (no grey, no gradients)**  
✅ **Confirm NO text, watermarks, or graphics in image**  

### DON'T:
❌ Use images where subjects are centered or left-positioned
❌ Use images where everyone wears the exact same color  
❌ Pick overly posed or stock-photo-feeling images  
❌ **Choose images with ANY background other than pure white**  
❌ Use images with harsh lighting or heavy shadows  
❌ Select images that feel too corporate or clinical  
❌ **Use images with ANY text, logos, or watermarks**
